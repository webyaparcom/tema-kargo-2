  2. 2

  1. 1

    Get the return code from the shop.

  3. 3

calculateHow is volumetric weight calculated?

  1. 1

    Multiply length, width and height in centimetres.

  2. 2

    Divide the result by 3000; that is the volumetric weight.

  3. 3

    The greater of volumetric weight and kilograms is charged.
